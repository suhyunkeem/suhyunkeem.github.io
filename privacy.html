Privacy Policy

AI 비서실장 · Effective November 18, 2026

  • This is a personal app used only by its operator with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.
  • It requests only the calendar.app.created scope and accesses only calendars it created. It does not access other calendars, email, contacts, or files.
  • Calendar data is used only to show and update the operator's own schedule, after the operator confirms each change.
  • Tokens and calendar copies are stored only on the operator's computer. Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked anytime at Google Account > Third-party access.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
